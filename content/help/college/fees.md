# Collecting fees and issuing receipts

Take a payment against a student's invoice at the counter, and hand over the
receipt as soon as it's issued.

## Steps

1. Open **Fees** from the sidebar and stay on the **Counter** tab.
2. Pick the **section**, then find the student with the search box (name or admission number).
3. Click **Take payment** on the invoice you're collecting against.
4. Enter the **amount**, choose the payment **mode** (cash, UPI, card, bank, gateway), and add a reference if there is one (UPI ref, cheque no.).
5. Confirm — a numbered receipt is issued immediately.
6. Click **Print receipt** to hand it over, or **Done** to close.

Use the **Collections** tab to total payments over a date range and reconcile the cash box. Admins use the **Setup** tab to add fee heads and generate invoices for a class.
