# Reading the analytics screen

Analytics shows trends, comparisons, and distributions for the classes,
subjects, or departments you're allowed to see — every figure is scoped to
your role.

## Steps

1. Open **Analytics** from the sidebar.
2. Review the attendance and marks trends for the areas you oversee.
3. Use the comparisons and distributions further down the page to spot classes or subjects that need attention.
4. Administrators: click **Recompute analytics** after a bulk data change, such as an import, so the figures catch up — otherwise they refresh on their own schedule.
