# Assigning and evaluating coursework

Subject teachers post assignments and study material here, and evaluate what
students submit.

## Steps

1. Open **Coursework** from the sidebar.
2. Pick the **class · subject** you teach.
3. To set an assignment, click **New assignment**, give it a title, a due date, and an optional max score.
4. To evaluate, open **Submissions** on an assignment and score each one.
5. To share notes or files, click **Upload material**, add a title, and attach a file (up to 1MB).
