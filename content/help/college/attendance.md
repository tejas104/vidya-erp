# Marking attendance

Attendance is marked per class session by the subject teacher or the class
teacher.

## Steps

1. Open **Attendance** from the sidebar.
2. Pick the class, section, and date.
3. Mark each student **present** or **absent**.
4. Save — once a session closes, the record is locked and further changes are audited, not overwritten.

Students below 75% attendance are flagged automatically on their profile.
