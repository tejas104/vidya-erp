# Applying for and approving leave

Staff apply for their own leave here and can track its status; approvers see
every pending request in the same queue.

## Steps

1. Open **Leave** from the sidebar.
2. To apply, click **Apply for leave**, pick the **From** and **To** dates, and submit.
3. Track your request's status — pending, approved, or rejected — on the same page.
4. Approvers: open a pending request in the queue and approve it, or **Reject** with a reason.
