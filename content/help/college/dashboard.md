# Signing in and your first steps

After you sign in, this is the first screen you land on. It's built for
your role, and a checklist walks new admins and teachers through initial
setup.

## Steps

1. Sign in with the username and password your college gave you.
2. On your first visits, follow the **checklist card** — it lists the setup steps still open for your role, such as adding classes or adding students.
3. Teachers: your dashboard opens on today's class first, showing the current period and its room.
4. Everyone: notices, at-risk students, and recent attendance and marks trends appear below the checklist.
5. Come back to the checklist any time — it stays out of the way once every step is checked off.

No Hindi or Marathi interface is available yet; the whole product is English-only for now.
