# Entering marks

Marks are entered per assessment by the subject teacher, one class and
subject at a time.

## Steps

1. Open **Marks** from the sidebar.
2. Pick the **class · subject** you teach from the dropdown.
3. Create an assessment — give it a name, a kind (e.g. unit test, midterm), and the maximum score.
4. Enter each student's score in the list; a score outside 0 and the maximum is flagged before you can save.
5. Save marks. You can jump between students with Enter on the keyboard.

Existing assessments for the class are listed below the form — open one to review or correct scores already entered.
