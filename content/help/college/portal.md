# Portal overview

The portal is the read-only view for a student or their linked
parent/guardian account — a summary of one student's attendance, marks, and
timetable.

## Steps

1. Sign in — a student or linked parent account lands on the portal automatically.
2. Check **My figures** for year-to-date attendance percentage and overall marks.
3. Look at **Today** for the current day's classes, teacher, and room.
4. Open **My timetable** for the full week's schedule.
5. Follow the first-steps checklist if it's still showing — it points out anything not yet set up, such as an enrollment that isn't linked.
