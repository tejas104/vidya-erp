# Adding and managing students

Students live inside sections — there is no single global list. Open a
section to see its roster, add a student, or update one already enrolled.

## Steps

1. Open **Students** from the sidebar.
2. Pick a **section** to see its roster.
3. Click **Add student**, fill in the admission number and full name, then **Create & enroll**.
4. To move a student to a different section, use **Transfer** on their row.
5. To retire a student, set their status (backlog, year back, transferred, dropped, alumni) instead of deleting them — the record is always kept.
6. Open a student's row to view their profile: a read-only summary of attendance and marks.

Adding students one at a time is best for occasional changes. For enrolling a whole class at once, use **Import → Students** instead — it accepts a CSV and validates it before writing anything.
