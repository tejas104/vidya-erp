# Generating and downloading reports

Reports are generated on demand, then downloaded once ready — every download
is re-checked against what you're allowed to see.

## Steps

1. Open **Reports** from the sidebar.
2. Choose the **report type**, then fill in whatever it needs (class, section, or student).
3. Choose the **format** — PDF, Excel (.xlsx), or CSV. PDF is the printable version of a grade card or hall ticket; Excel and CSV contain its rows for analysis.
4. Click **Generate report**. Larger reports run in the background and appear as "pending" until they finish.
5. Click **Refresh** if a report you just generated hasn't appeared yet, then download it from the list.
