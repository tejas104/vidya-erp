# Publishing notices

Post a notice to the whole college, the staff room, or a single department
or class — readers only ever see what's addressed to them.

## Steps

1. Open **Notices** from the sidebar.
2. Click **New notice**.
3. Enter a **title** and the notice body, and choose who it's for.
4. Optionally set a publish date (leave blank to publish immediately) and an expiry date.
5. Save — the notice appears on readers' noticeboards right away, or on the publish date you chose.

To take a notice down early, open it from the board and use **Take this notice down**.
