# School assessments and marks

Use this screen to create a term-linked assessment and enter scores for the class, section, and subject you teach.

## Who can use this

Only a teacher assigned to the selected subject and class can create its assessments or save marks. Access is checked by the server for the class and subject; opening the page does not grant access to another teacher's class or subject.

## Before you start

You need a teaching assignment for the class, section, and subject. An administrator must have created an open academic term and configured its assessment types. A grade scale for the school must be available. The assessment date must be within the selected term, and the section roster must contain the students whose scores you enter.

## Create an assessment

1. Open **Marks** from the sidebar.
2. Choose **Class, section and subject** that you teach.
3. Choose an open **Term**.
4. Under **Create assessment**, enter the **Assessment name**, choose an **Assessment type**, enter a positive **Maximum score**, choose the **Assessment date**, and select the **Grade scale**.
5. Select **Create assessment**.

The assessment appears in the **Assessments** table and its marks entry form opens. The first assessment fixes the term's selected grade scale and weighting plan; later scale edits do not change grades already recorded for that term.

If there are no terms, ask an administrator to create the term, assessment types, and grade scale. If the form cannot be submitted, confirm that the date is inside the term, the maximum score is greater than zero, and the selected assessment type and grade scale are available.

## Enter or correct marks

1. In the **Assessments** table, select **Open marks** for the required assessment.
2. Enter each learner's score. A score may be zero, but it cannot exceed the assessment's maximum and supports at most two decimal places.
3. Select **Save marks**.

Vidya saves the scores and the grades calculated from the assessment's saved grade scale. The **Recorded grades** table shows the saved score, percentage, and grade. If saving fails, the scores remain in the form so you can review the error and select **Save marks** again.

If a score is rejected, check that the learner is enrolled in the assessment class for the Academic Year and that the score is within the maximum. If you do not see a teaching subject or get an access message, ask the administrator to verify the staff teaching assignment; do not enter marks through another teacher's account.

## Closed terms

When a term is closed, its assessments and recorded grades remain visible but marks are read-only. An administrator must reopen the term with a reason before any correction can be saved. This is a school term rule and does not change legacy college marks behavior.
