# Your family view

Use **Family** after the school invites you and you activate your guardian account. Each linked child appears separately. The school controls which record categories your relationship may open.

## Steps

1. Choose a child by name if more than one is linked to your account.
2. Review that child's attendance, marks, and today’s timetable when available. **Not recorded** means the school has not entered that figure.
3. Open **Fees** for issued invoice balances and receipts. The amounts are shown in rupees; the school records payments.
4. Read **School notices** for currently published school and class messages. Staff-only messages do not appear here.
5. To link another child, enter the invitation code supplied by the school. A link awaiting staff verification will say so before any records appear.

If a section cannot load, use its **Retry** button. If a child or category is missing, contact the school office to check the relationship or invitation; another child's records are never opened by guessing a link.
