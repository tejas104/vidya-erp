# School reports

Create a report from records you are allowed to read. Every download checks your current access again.

## Generate a report

1. Choose a report type. Student, section, and class reports ask for their relevant target.
2. For **Teacher attendance — daily register**, choose the school and attendance date. This report is available to administrators and principals and includes the full dated teacher register, even when the Attendance page is searched or paged.
3. Choose PDF for printing, Excel (.xlsx) for a workbook, or CSV for data exchange.
4. Select **Generate report**. The file is prepared in the background. Use **Refresh** to update its status, then select **Download**.

An unmarked teacher appears as **Not marked** in the register. **On leave** is a manually recorded presence status and does not approve a leave request.
