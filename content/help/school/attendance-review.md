# Review attendance shortfall

Choose the term, section and through date, then select **Review attendance**. The school must first configure that term's instructional days and threshold under **Academic terms**.

**Registers missing** counts scheduled days without a class teacher whole-day register. A subject teacher's period does not fill that gap. **Need data** includes pupils with a missing scheduled day or missing pupil entry; their percentage and shortfall decision are withheld. **Confirmed below threshold** counts only pupils with complete daily records.

The current section roster is assumed enrolled throughout the selected term window because effective enrollment dates are not stored. Confirm mid-term admissions or transfers with the office before using a figure for a decision. This screen does not automatically impose a consequence on a pupil.
