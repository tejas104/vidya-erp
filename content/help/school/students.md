# Student record

Open a student from a class roster, the student directory, search, or the needs-attention list. The header shows the admission number, lifecycle status, and current class and section from the student record.

## Tabs

- **Summary** shows recorded profile fields and performance you are allowed to read. A denied performance panel does not hide the student profile.
- **Academics** lists marks for the current academic year.
- **Attendance** lists visible sessions and their recorded statuses.
- **Finance** lists invoices and dues. Only staff with fee scope can read it.
- **Documents** lists files on record; use the student management screen to add or remove files if authorized.
- **Family** shows guardian invitations and relationships for authorized staff.
- **History** keeps enrollment rows, including withdrawn rows, status changes, and their audit events.

Each tab has its own loading, error, and access state. **Not recorded** means there is no source value; **Not in your scope** means that source is not available to your account. A student's record and history are never hard-deleted as part of routine school work.
