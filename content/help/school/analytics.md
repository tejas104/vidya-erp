# School analytics

Analytics shows attendance and academic figures for the classes or school areas you are allowed to oversee. Figures from outside your access are not included.

## Attendance trend

Use **Columns** to compare monthly percentages, **Line** to see their direction, **Area** to emphasize the change, or **Data** for exact month-by-month values. Line and Area appear after at least two months have recorded data. The sentence above the chart states the latest percentage and change from the first recorded month.

Other panels compare areas and show marks only when enough permitted data exists. A withheld figure is different from a recorded zero.

Administrators can select **Recompute analytics** after a bulk data change. The figures may take a moment to refresh.
