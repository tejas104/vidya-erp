# Your student portal

Use **My register** after the school links your student record to your sign-in. The page shows your current enrollment, attendance, classes and school term marks.

## Read term marks

1. Open **My register** and find **My term marks**.
2. Choose a term when more than one is available. Only released, closed terms with assessments for your class appear. Open terms stay private while teachers enter and correct scores.
3. Read each subject's percentage and the count of recorded assessments. Choose **View assessments** to see individual scores and maximum marks. If a mark is missing, that subject says **Incomplete** and the overall result is unavailable. Missing marks are never treated as zero.

An administrator may reopen a term to make corrections. Its marks disappear from this view until the term is closed again. An issued report-card PDF has a separate school publication process and may show a frozen result from an earlier date.

If your sign-in is not linked to a student record, ask the school office to link it. If an expected closed term does not appear, ask the office to check your enrollment and term status.
