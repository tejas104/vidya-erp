# Your school workspace

The first screen after sign-in shows work you can do with your assigned school role. Your menu and pupil data follow your permissions.

## Where to start

1. **Administrator:** open student records, fees, report cards, or teacher assignments from **Your workspaces**.
2. **Principal:** open school analytics, report cards, leave decisions, or notices. Pending leave requests appear first when there are requests to decide.
3. **Class teacher:** take whole-class attendance, open your class roster, and review term records for the classes assigned to you.
4. **Subject teacher:** use the teaching timetable and attendance shortcut, then open marks, coursework, or syllabus coverage.

**School pulse** shows attendance only when a rollup is available. **Pupils to review** follows the current analytics and links to the pupil record. A missing or failed read is shown as unavailable, not as zero. Use the term-record or marks link to open the school's assessment data; the dashboard does not calculate a school grade average.

The noticeboard shows notices visible to your role. If a class or teaching period is missing, ask the school administrator to check your assignment.
